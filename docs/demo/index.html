Discovery & saved products+
  • Instant product search with keyboard navigation, category scope, SKU lookup and bounded typo tolerance.
  • Browser-based guest wishlist, a dedicated saved-items page and live count beside the native header cart. Saved items stay in that browser.
  • Top-level category navigation, a configurable promotion strip and coupon-code copy button.
  • Promotion background color control with automatic text contrast and a reset to the parent accent.
Product confidence & honest merchandising+
  • Real product review averages and counts, a featured approved review, and configurable purchase reassurance.
  • Sale percentages calculated from actual catalog prices; low-stock messages based on WooCommerce inventory.
  • Sale countdowns only when a real sale end is scheduled. Configured offers can have their own discount and duration.
  • Desktop sticky add-to-cart. On mobile, the product sticky bar is hidden; optional sticky controls enhance the native cart and checkout actions.
  • A product-content readiness checklist for images and descriptions, plus policy/footer links.
Cart, checkout & editable helpful content+
  • Native mini-cart opening after a product is added, and shipping progress that follows live cart totals.
  • Visible Required/Optional field labels and factual checkout guidance.
  • Publish helpful checkout content with WordPress core blocks: headings, text, images, columns, lists and buttons. Draft content stays hidden.
  • Selected native product reviews in static cards or an accessible rotator. Verified-owner labels follow WooCommerce records; autoplay is optional.
  • Checkout add-ons and helpful content target the WooCommerce Checkout block, rather than the legacy checkout shortcode.
Checkout add-ons, upsells & downsells+
  • Up to four optional product add-ons above Place order. Nothing is preselected; cart totals update without clearing entered details.
  • Optional percentage discounts on each add-on, with catalog-backed prices, clear savings labels and retained accepted prices.
  • Configured upsell/downsell offers in three placements: full-page before checkout, passive inline before Place order, or after purchase.
  • Offer decisions apply to the current cart and configuration for guests and signed-in shoppers. Changed carts can be reconsidered; accepted offers are not repeatedly added.
  • Post-purchase acceptance starts a separate checkout and order. It does not silently recharge or modify the completed order.
Social proof, settings & after purchase+
  • Recent-purchase proof uses eligible WooCommerce orders. It defaults to off and anonymous; displaying names is a separate privacy choice.
  • Review shortcodes have independent selections, rating filters, attribution choices and previews.
  • A post-purchase account invitation lets shoppers create an account after checkout.
  • Guided settings for Storefront, Product pages, Cart & checkout, Offers and Social proof. Section saves retain unrelated settings.
  • No fabricated purchases, viewer counts or stock numbers. Feature availability is not a promise of a measured conversion lift.

Read the walkthrough transcript
  1. Purple’s white storefront, typography and purple purchase buttons stay familiar.
  2. Category navigation and native catalog filters help shoppers explore the range.
  3. Instant search offers matching products as the shopper types.
  4. Review summaries and purchase guidance sit near the product’s buy button.
  5. A saved product appears on the browser wishlist with a live header count.
  6. The native mini-cart opens after an item is added.
  7. The cart shows shipping progress alongside the native checkout action.
  8. A configured upsell gives the shopper a clear accept or decline decision.
  9. Choose before-checkout, inline or post-purchase offers, with an optional downsell. This is a feature explanation card.
  10. Optional checkout add-ons show the configured products and current prices.
  11. Published helpful content and selected reviews add context beside the order summary.
  12. The mobile checkout retains the native purchase control.
  13. Post-purchase offers use a separate checkout; the completed order stays unchanged.
  14. ConvertSell and its optional toolkit are separate, free GPL packages built on Purple.
